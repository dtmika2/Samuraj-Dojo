⚔ Samurai Dojo

Topple as many cardboard samurai as you can in 60 seconds.

TIME60
SCORE0
BEST0
Waiting for Figmin XR…
  1. Tap the controller connect icon on this window first (until then the katana is locked — otherwise the trigger would make copies of it).
  2. Grab the katana floating next to this window and swing it.
  3. Every few seconds a shuriken floats up near you. Grab it with the grip button (middle finger) and throw it with a real swing — let go to release.
  4. Cardboard samurai = 1 point. Colourful armoured shogun = 3 points.
  5. Spare the monk and the geisha — toppling one costs 2 points.
  6. Don't hit the black bomb — it ends the game instantly.
